<objective>
Close the cross-AI review finding [HIGH] "Header after-size fields are parsed outside the declared OBU
boundary" (`.planning/reviews/2026-09-13-CODEBASE-REVIEWS.md:56`, consolidated item 1 at `:238`).

Today `read_obu_header_parts` (`src/obu/header.rs:415-478`) validates the numeric `obu_size` and then reads
the trim pair and the extension header from the parent cursor, bounded only by the end of the input.
`read_extension_header` (`:487-493`) copies up to `bytes_remaining()` of the parent, so a 2-byte OBU can
allocate a 1 MiB extension. The underflow is only caught later in `read_obu_with_header`
(`src/obu/mod.rs:139-142`), and the public header-only `read_obu_header` never catches it.

The fix reads the after-size fields through a non-advancing window of `min(obu_size, bytes_remaining)`
bytes. An overrun of `obu_size` becomes `TruncatedObu` at the OBU start, which is exactly the kind and offset
`read_obu_with` already reports for this defect. Other window errors are rebased to absolute offsets.

Conformance rule (both spec and references, stricter wins): the spec says `obu_size` SHALL include the
after-size fields (IAMF v1.1.0 `index.bs:548`). iamf-tools@v2.1.0 `ObuHeader::ReadAndValidate` rejects a
negative remaining payload, but only after reading and resizing. libiamf@v1.1.0 `IAMF_OBU_split` wraps the
unsigned payload size and does not reject. Rejecting inside the bound satisfies all three.

Purpose: parser hardening. No bitstream-visible output, no reference hash and no golden byte changes
(research Q4: 110 of 110 committed inputs give identical `parse_sequence` and `dump_annotated` output).

Output: a bounded header reader, a corrected citation, 9 new header tests plus 1 tightened test, and two
comment/doc touch-ups.
