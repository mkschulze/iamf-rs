# Phase quick-260914-5c5 Plan 01: Tolerate opaque extension param definitions; refuse non-round-tripping writer states Summary

Seven low-level writer states that wrote `Ok` but re-read differently are now refused before emission with two appended unit `ErrorKind`s.
